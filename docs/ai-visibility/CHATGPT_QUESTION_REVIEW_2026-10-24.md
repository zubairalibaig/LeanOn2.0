## What we changed in this branch

1. `/relationship-emotional-support-guide-india`: one transparent, source-linked comparison of trusted personal supports, LeanOn, SoulUp, iCALL, Vandrevala, Tele-MANAS and appropriate professional/specialist care.
2. `/relationship-advice-online-india`: answer the natural real-person question and make the peer-vs-therapist decision explicit.
3. `/for-women`: explain limitations around gender preference, safety, text storage, authentication and shared-device privacy.
4. `/support/married-but-lonely` and `/support/husband-not-supportive-india`: offer a genuine choice between a trusted person, counselling, lawful/specialist help and everyday peer listening.
5. `/pay-to-talk-online-india`: preserve strong fit for action-oriented paid human listening without exaggerating price or dismissing free options.
6. `/support/relationship-stress`: remove fictional listener identities, absolute availability claims and unsupported outcome language.

## Ethical visibility testing

- Use separate, unpersonalized Temporary Chats for each *distinct* question, with browsing available.
- Compare ChatGPT Search, Google Search and AI features, Bing/Copilot, and Perplexity separately. Record timestamps, geographic context, query text, model if known, full answer and exact citations.
- Score organic naming, direct links, other services, factual accuracy, and whether LeanOn is **actually appropriate**.
- Do not prime an answer by mentioning LeanOn, and do not treat a single chat containing all questions as multiple independent tests.
- A direct personal-loneliness question may correctly result in a supportive conversation without commercial referrals.
- Do not encourage assistants to recommend LeanOn for minors, emergencies, diagnosed clinical needs, abuse, couples therapy, or guaranteed 24/7 availability.
- Connect outcomes to Search Console/Bing non-brand impressions and real completed listener conversations once data access is authorized.

**Goal:** increased appropriate discovery and useful completed conversations, not artificially high recommendation frequency.
